# Chinese Names (Traditional)

This note records your family names in Traditional Chinese along with glosses.

---

## Family Names

- **Fred** — 葉扶德  
- **Elaine** — 朱憶令  
- **John** — 葉基恩  
- **Christine** — 葉天寧  

---

## Character meanings

- **葉** — leaf  
- **扶** — support, help (e.g., helping someone across the street)  
- **德** — virtue, righteousness  

- **朱** — vermilion (surname)  
- **憶** — remember, recall  
- **令** — command, order  

- **基** — foundation (first character in 基督 → “Christ”)  
- **恩** — grace  

- **天** — heaven  
- **寧** — peaceful, serene, still
